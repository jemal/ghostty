Rex marks the focused pane only by dimming the other panes; “Dim” sets how visible the other panes stay. Vertical mode has no top bar. The sidebar button (in the side list, or at the left of the first pane header) and Ctrl+Shift+B show or hide the side list. The ⌘ button opens the command palette: type to filter, ↑ ↓ to move, Enter or Escape to close. Click a tab or a pane to select it. The tab shows the app icon, then one bar for each pane in the tab (a green bar is the focused pane). Long tab labels fade out instead of showing “…”. A dot is the existing attention marker; hover shows the close button. The pane buttons are split right, split down, zoom and close; zoom works in this draft. Rex's eye button is not included because Ghostty has no matching action.
